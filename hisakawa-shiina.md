# HISAKAWA SHIINA
-# [久川 椎名] — The Gentle Blade

### ❀ GENERAL INFORMATION

- **Name:** Hisakawa Shiina  
- **Age:** 22 years old  
- **Gender:** Female  
- **Occupation:** Wandering Swordswoman

### ❀ ORIGINS & PERSONALITY

Shiina grew up in a small coastal village with her older brother, **Shimakuro**. As children, they spent much of their time exploring the shoreline or practicing swordsmanship together. Their maternal aunt is also the mother of **Shinohara Mei**, making Mei their cousin.

Shiina has always been **gentle, caring, and a little too trusting**. She tends to see the best in people, sometimes overlooking things that others would find suspicious.

Around strangers, she is usually quiet and somewhat shy. Once she gets to know someone, however, she becomes much more comfortable and talkative. This is especially true around Shimakuro, whom she enjoys teasing whenever he acts a little too serious.

Although her trusting nature occasionally causes trouble, she refuses to believe that kindness is something she should give up.

She knows the world is not always kind, but that has never stopped her from trying to be.

### ❀ THE MASTER'S TEACHINGS

Shiina and her brother were trained by an elderly swordmaster who lived near their village. Besides teaching them how to wield a sword, he often reminded them that patience and restraint were just as important as skill.

One day, he asked Shiina what she would do if her lord ordered her to harm an innocent person.

She thought about it for a moment before answering that she would try to reason with her lord and convince him to reconsider.

Her master simply replied:

> *"Then may you always have the courage to seek another way."*

Shiina has remembered those words ever since.

She understands that fighting is sometimes unavoidable, but she would rather exhaust every peaceful option before drawing her sword.

### ❀ HONOR & LOYALTY

Shiina takes her promises seriously and remains devoted to any province she chooses to serve. She believes that loyalty is important, particularly when others are depending on her.

She also has great respect for the **Shogun**. Having been raised to value duty and order, she finds it difficult to imagine someone in such a position deliberately acting against the people they are meant to protect.

If she received an order she believed was unjust, she would first try to understand the reason behind it. She would rather speak up respectfully than immediately turn against her lord.

Unfortunately, her willingness to trust others sometimes makes it difficult for her to recognize dishonesty.

Shiina admires Shimakuro's determination, even if she occasionally wishes he were less stubborn.

She worries that his strong sense of justice might someday put him in danger, though she knows convincing him to change his mind is rarely easy.

Despite their different views, **Shimakuro remains the person she trusts most**.

### ❀ THE PLACE YOU RETURN TO

Whenever she has the chance, Shiina returns to the coastal village where she grew up.

On a grassy hill overlooking the sea lies the grave of the swordmaster who taught her and her brother.

She visits regularly when she is home, bringing fresh flowers or taking care of the ones already growing around the grave.

Sometimes she speaks to him about her travels, sharing little stories as though he were still there to listen. Other days, she simply sits nearby and watches the waves.

Shimakuro occasionally joins her. They often end up reminiscing about their childhood, laughing over old memories or recalling the little habits their master used to have.

Shiina enjoys these quiet afternoons more than she likes to admit.

She especially likes it when her brother has nowhere else to be. He is usually willing to listen to her ramble, even if he occasionally pretends otherwise.

No matter how far her travels take her, she always finds herself missing the familiar sound of the sea and the people waiting back home.
